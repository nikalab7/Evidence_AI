How an answer is built

  1. Search

    Semantic search finds the most relevant passages in the Wikipedia corpus and your uploaded documents.

  2. Connect

    The agent runs up to four follow-up searches to link facts that live in different documents.

  3. Check citations

    Each displayed claim includes a verbatim quote from retrieved evidence. Quote matching checks presence, not whether the source logically entails the claim.
